# WOR-92: core symmetry, Jeans resolution, and local AMR controls (2026-11-07)

## Finding

The root-32³, `mesh/numlevel=2` CFL=0.3 solver pilot is numerically converged by its Newton gate, but its central collapse is **not spatially resolved**. The global minimum Jeans length falls below four cell widths at code time 1827.36, then below two at 1931.46. It is 1.453 cells in the shared cycle-74 checkpoint and 0.900 cells at cycle 82, while `problem/N_J=32` requests 32 cells. The mesh is already at its configured AMR ceiling (history logical level 3; HDF5 level 1). The later one-octant density peak must not be interpreted as a resolved first core.

The central eight cells at `(±93.75, ±93.75, ±93.75) AU` are symmetric through cycle 79. Their density max/min rises to 1.2723 at cycle 80 (`t=2082.3698`), 2.291 at cycle 81, and 5.418 at cycle 82. Previous output shows ratios 173 at cycle 100 and 462 at cycle 120. At the matched cycle-80 time, CFL=0.15 has ratio 1.0000007, but it develops asymmetry later; reducing CFL delays the onset in this capped-resolution calculation. One-rank and two-rank histories are byte-identical. Turning off the ambient temperature reset gives ratio 1.2729. Replacing the Semenov table with constant `kappa_P=kappa_R=1` gives 1.2702. These controls show that the onset is not explained solely by MPI decomposition, the ambient reset, or the Semenov lookup. They do **not** prove that the hydro/gravity algorithm is correct or identify a unique cause.

Its first post-refinement dump at cycle 75 has 48 new finest-level cells with pressure near the configured `1e-10` code-unit floor, while the original mesh has none. Its reported minimum Jeans length is transiently `2.4e-5` cells and its later density is 12 times the original at the same time. Regridding only after the Jeans length is already 1.45 cells changes the state discontinuously. There is no evidence here that adding one level late repairs the original trajectory.

## Earlier AMR test

Starting from the symmetric cycle-36 checkpoint (`t=1018.2071`, minimum Jeans length 22.16 cells), `mesh/numlevel=3` ran through cycle 63 (`t=1449.4041`). It created 392 blocks, ending with 512, minimum Jeans length 27.50 cells, and central density ratio 1.00000008. All 29 recorded Newton stages converged and no opacity lookup was clamped. The worst logged FGMRES true relative residual is `2.189e-8`, just above the nominal `2e-8` inner target but below the bounded inexact cap `1e-7`; the nonlinear stage converged. The run was stopped with a handled SIGTERM after cycle 63 to obtain a restart before the local AMR ceiling became severely underresolved. The restart's exact cycle-63 HDF5 dump has no near-floor pressure cell.

From that cycle-63 restart, raising the cap to `mesh/numlevel=4` for one step created 224 additional blocks (736 total) and restored the minimum Jeans length from 27.50 to 31.96 cells at cycle 64. The central density ratio remained 1.00000008. Newton converged in two iterations; both FGMRES true residuals were below `2e-8`; accepted-state opacity and trial table diagnostics were zero. Its HDF5 dump has no near-floor pressure cell. This checks one local regrid/solver step, not a long trajectory or first-core resolution.

Six further steps from cycle 64 to 70 on that 736-block mesh used the [cycle-output input](wor92_core_symmetry_cycle_output_20261107.in) (SHA-256 `ff88b2b3822f610e9a290a7c0c824ec1f16315f5b8d1f25986fdaef888be57cb`) with `output2/dt=0 output4/dt=0` to clear time-output settings retained by the restart. The actual dt declined from 7.035 to 6.888 code units. At `t=1505.3938`, minimum Jeans length was 31.916 cells and the central density ratio was 1.00000019. All seven logged Newton stages converged, maximum FGMRES true residual was `1.828e-8`, and there were zero opacity clamps and near-floor pressure cells. This is a short local validation of timely refinement and the solver; continuation into the deeply collapsing regime requires substantially more AMR capacity and memory.
