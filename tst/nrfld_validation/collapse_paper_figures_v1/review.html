最終状態の半径分布（主図候補）

Density and mass-weighted radial velocity show a compact, slowly moving inner region bounded by an accretion-shock candidate at 7.507–7.874 au (shading, inferred from the +x-axis profile). Gas temperature is mass weighted; the radiation-equivalent temperature is derived from the shell-mean radiation energy density.

The domain maxima of gas density and temperature rise to 1.044×10⁻¹⁰ g cm⁻³ and 434.524 K at 29.110 kyr. The right panel combines the two maxima at each output time; it is not a trajectory of one fluid element.

図を選ぶときの注意

  • 主図は半径分布と進化の2枚を推奨。xy断面は3D形状を示したい場合の補助図。
  • 背景ガス・放射場は固定処理があるため、全エネルギー保存を主張しない。

  • 第一コアまでを対象とし、H₂解離・第二コアは範囲外。
