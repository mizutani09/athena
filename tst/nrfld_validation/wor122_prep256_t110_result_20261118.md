## Result and interpretation

XD job **341450** completed normally in 09:30:03 (2026-11-17 15:36 to
2026-11-18 01:06 JST). Both Athena stages terminated at their requested
time limits, and the opacity diagnostic reported zero out-of-domain,

The global Mach growth is in the density-floor exterior, not at the
stellar surface. In 0–700 R☉ the axis and diagonal radial RMS speeds
agree within a few percent. At 700–830 R☉ the axis/diagonal ratio is
about 2, although the shell radial speeds remain below about 0.32 km/s
RMS. Thus there is outer grid anisotropy, but no evidence here that a
rapid axial core jet caused the global Mach increase.

The preparation source supplies approximately the entire stellar
luminosity on ordinary time steps. Its t=110 terminal-row ratio of 0.706
is misleading because that last step is only 0.00114 code time; the
ordinary-step median is 0.995 and the last ordinary value is 1.005.
The source keeps the 800–813 R☉ gas temperature within ~0.5% of t=52,
while measured 800–830 R☉ enthalpy covariance decreases. The latter is
a shell-volume proxy, not an exact flux through a spherical face.
The model has therefore **not** become self-sustaining despite reaching
the optimistic local `H/v` time at r≈813 R☉. This does not prove that
resolution alone is responsible: the initial 1D transport, turbulent
support, and unresolved opacity/temperature transition remain coupled.
