## 2026-11-08: why the high-resolution run reached the limit

The 960-block XD run has two **distinct** linear-solve regimes. The earlier local WOR-92 improvements made the true-residual gate bounded and the nonlinear solve safer; they did not prove that every high-resolution FGMRES solve would finish below a fixed 300-iteration cap. The code intentionally rejects a true residual above the opt-in `1e-7` cap. At cycle 13 the `1.2641885398e-6` residual was rejected, rather than silently passed to Newton.

### Cycle 13: restart-40 / MG2 convergence

From the *same saved cycle-13 state* (`job_337766/output/cfl030_flux_precision16_local.00004.rst`, 960 blocks), the 64-rank restart-40/MG2 control 337789 fails after 300 iterations at true relative residual `8.2880663387e-7`; restart-80/MG2 job 337790 completes that cycle with all three solves below `2e-8` and at most 163 iterations. The 32-rank control 337773 also fails at 300 iterations (`1.1830016244e-6`) from the cycle-10 checkpoint. At 64 ranks, increasing MG V-cycles from two to three with restart 40 (337774) likewise completes through cycle 14 with at most 230 iterations. These bounded controls identify inadequate restarted-Krylov/preconditioner convergence for this state, rather than an MPI-rank-specific error. The changed solver parameters alter the trajectory slightly; the saved-cycle-13 pair minimizes that confound.

The opt-in correction trace binary (`9885712327eb7bcffac75338d2eda7b4161ec8cf2f4468d6d5ef08d6211d8640`) is based on diagnostic branch `codex/wor92-xd-steptrace-20261108`, commit `2d056f3434631f60ec367d1d693b6796d9289800`. At cycle 13, job 337809 reproduces the uninstrumented 337789 true residual exactly. At 300 iterations it reports projected `8.2880820166e-7`, independently predicted `8.2880820158e-7`, true `8.2880663387e-7`, and operator gap `9.55065106e-10`. The gap is about 0.12% of the true residual. This failure is not the finite-precision correction barrier documented for the earlier local cycle-0 problem.

### Cycle 28–29: finite-precision correction barrier

Restart-80/MG2 passes cycles 10–20 with the strict `2e-8` target (jobs 337775/337785). Job 337788 then reaches cycle 30, but **2 of its 20 FGMRES solves are inexact accepts**: at cycles 28 and 29, each reaches 600 iterations with true relative residual `5.4912099966e-8` and `3.8199355257e-8`, respectively. Both are below the opt-in `1e-7` inexact cap and the outer Newton stages converge, but neither is a strict linear convergence. The original interpretation of this job as strictly converged was incorrect and has been corrected in the user-facing record.

Strict controls from the same saved cycle-28 state (`job_337788/output/cfl030_flux_precision16_local.00019.rst`) confirm the limit: restart-80/MG2 (337802) stops at 600 and `4.5810759357e-8`; restart-40/MG2 (337804) stops at 600 and `4.4195440142e-8`. Restart-80/MG3 (337803) strictly converges the cycle-28 solves in 61 and 100 iterations, but a two-cycle continuation fails the cycle-29 second solve at both 300 iterations (`3.3816151651e-8`, 337806) and 600 iterations (`3.4165952869e-8`, 337807). No tested MG/restart configuration demonstrated strict convergence through both cycles.

The non-mutating step trace at cycle 28 (337808) reproduces the uninstrumented 337802 solve exactly. At 600 iterations, the projected and independently predicted relative residuals are `1.6533618916e-8` (strictly below target), while the true residual after storing the correction in double precision is `4.5810759357e-8`. The operator gap is `4.3003504829e-8`, of which the correction-representation gap is `4.2153340780e-8`; the remaining operator-evaluation gap is `8.7882288239e-9`. The requested correction norm is `4.84544e-13`, and its representation difference has norm `6.88880e-19`, which the diffusion-dominated operator amplifies. The squared gap is distributed across interiors, same-level faces and coarse-fine faces; it is not confined to AMR interfaces. This is the same finite-precision mechanism as the earlier [roundoff audit](../wor92_roundoff_mechanism_20261107.md), now measured on the XD high-resolution state.

The 337800 P-large-a continuation was canceled while pending, before execution, after the cycle-28/29 non-strict accepts were identified. Job 337772 had also been canceled before execution when switching to restart 80. There is currently **no WOR-92 XD long job running or queued**. The strict full-collapse route needs a numerically justified operator/state precision or scaling/preconditioning improvement, followed by strict Newton, conservation, opacity-domain and matched-time resolution checks. A bounded inexact-Newton long pilot may be considered separately, but the `1e-7` gate and the current history precision do not by themselves establish a physical accuracy budget.
