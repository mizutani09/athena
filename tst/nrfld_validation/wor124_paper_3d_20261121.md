# WOR-124 3-D collapse density and magnetic field (2026-11-21)

## Source and method

The final AMR+1 rotating, weak-field table-EOS state was rendered in three
dimensions without launching another simulation.  The source is cycle 2400
from M-large-a job 344378:

- source tag and solver commit: `protostellar-firstcore-v2`,
  `577f273bddee4128d873cd7da7a4b1495b97ba8c`;
- HDF SHA-256:
  `943f4714a639b95f30bbd597a09dd95dca1cb0c9d9f7fcb73ebbf269847af59a`;
- 4,852 leaf MeshBlocks, maximum AMR level index 11;
- native maxima: `rho=1.0041786e-10 g cm^-3` and `|B|=1.7758467 G`.

`paper_collapse_3d.py` samples AMR leaf-cell values piecewise-constantly onto
a uniform display lattice.  It produces translucent density isosurfaces,
density volume rendering, and Runge--Kutta integrations of the normalized
sampled cell-centred magnetic field.  The products are provided as static PNG,
a 72-frame H.264 orbit, a self-contained interactive HTML scene, and VTK
VTI/VTP/VTM files for further inspection in ParaView.

Two complementary scales were generated at 128 cubed display points:

- core: +/-50 au, 0.78125 au display spacing, density levels
  `log10(rho)=-14.5,-13.5,-12.5,-11.5,-10.5`;
- envelope: +/-300 au, 4.6875 au display spacing, density levels
  `log10(rho)=-15.5,-14.8,-14.1,-13.4,-12.7`.

The core raster recovers `rho_max=9.0982e-11 g cm^-3` and
`|B|_max=1.6942 G`; the coarser envelope raster recovers
`rho_max=1.0672e-11 g cm^-3` and `|B|_max=0.65887 G`.  The lower envelope
maxima are a display-resolution effect, which is why the core and envelope
views must be interpreted together.

## Interpretation and limits

The core view shows a flattened high-density object together with substantial
field winding.  The envelope view connects this structure to the more nearly
vertical outer field and shows the large-scale pinch.  These are useful
three-dimensional morphology views and are consistent with the xy/xz slices;
they do not add a new quantitative physical claim.

The displayed curves are not native constrained-transport field lines.  They
integrate a normalized, uniformly resampled, cell-centred B field.  Therefore
they must not be used to measure magnetic flux, divB, reconnection, or unique
field-line topology.  The quantitative magnetic-energy, plasma-beta,
conservation, and divB results remain those from the native HDF/history audits.
AMR stair-stepping and the uniform display lattice can also appear in the
isosurfaces.  The VTK files are provided so camera angle, opacity, and seed
placement can be changed without rerunning the collapse simulation.
