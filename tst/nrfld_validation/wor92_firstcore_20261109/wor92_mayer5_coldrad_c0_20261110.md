# Mayer §5 pressure-equilibrium ambient with cold initial radiation

The gas initial conditions follow the reference [§5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12620#S5) and its cited [precursor §3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.07016#S3): a uniform 1 M☉ / 3000 au / 14 K core, ambient density 1/30 of the core, and initial gas-pressure equilibrium. The implied ideal-gas background temperature is 420 K. The papers do not specify how radiation energy is initialized in the dilute background. The LTE assumption `Er=a_r(420 K)^4` produced background radiation pressure 30,566 times the gas pressure and a first-step Newton failure in job 339297. To test whether this is an avoidable *radiation initialization* artifact, the [new input](wor92_mayer5_reference_coldrad_c0_20261110.in) initializes and fixes background radiation at **14 K**, while keeping background gas at 420 K and its pressure equal to the core. This assumption is explicit and must not be presented as a documented Mayer parameter.

The source change in `protostellar_core_fld.cpp` adds optional `problem/background_radiation_temperature`, defaulting to the old background gas temperature. Only when set does it change the initial and fixed background radiation field. It does not modify the FLD residual, Jacobian, hydro solver or core gas initial conditions. The previous repaired ideal binary is left untouched. XD M-debug build **339301** completed `0:0` in 1:52; binary SHA-256 `09796ca13c0547413073c3cd599f3e52447106219ff04fd43174aae5e177b544`.

M-test-a c0→10 job **339305** failed after 22 seconds. Its c0→1 Newton solve converged in four iterations, but at the start of c1→2 `NRFLD::CalculateCoefficientsOnce` rejected radiation values between `−1.0e-18` and `−3.0e-17` in `i=1/10` or `j/k=1/10` halo cells. A c0→1 diagnostic **339308** completed `0:0` in 22 seconds; its cycle-1 3D HDF5 has **no negative active radiation cells** and an active minimum `Er=0.29064393` code. Thus the failure is a halo interpolation undershoot at roundoff scale, not a negative accepted physical radiation state.

With `background_sink_after_implicit=false`, the accepted cycle-1 background gas had cooled despite its fixed-temperature setting. Setting it to `true` in [the revised input](wor92_mayer5_reference_postreset_c0_20261110.in) restored the accepted background to 420 K, but its c0→10 preflight **339307** also stopped after 18 seconds at the same tiny negative halo values (the c0→1 Newton solve still converged). These are intentionally retained as failed controls. The next test adds a limited halo-only roundoff correction under the existing positive NR ghost mode; see [the halo experiment](wor92_mayer5_halo_c0_20261110.md).
