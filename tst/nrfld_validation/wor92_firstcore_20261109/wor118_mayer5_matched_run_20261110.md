Because the EOS is selected at compile time, build the identical source in a separate XD directory with `--eos=adiabatic`; preserve the validated table-EOS binary.

It is closer to [Mayer et al. §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12620#S5) than the table run, but still uses Athena++ Cartesian Jeans AMR and its own ambient pressure implementation rather than AREPO's moving mesh, mass refinement, and published background treatment. ### Controlled local-gas repair

The input set `exact_local_gas_solve=true`, but `src/nr_multigrid/NRFLD.cpp` compiled that branch only when `GENERAL_EOS && EOS_TABLE_ENABLED`. With `--eos=adiabatic`, `GENERAL_EOS=0`, so the requested exact solve was inactive. Commit `582acd5999c1a5681ab1bc1d1af292676642ed40` on isolated branch `codex/wor118-ideal-local-gas-20261110` allows the same monotone local matter equation solve for the ideal EOS, using its existing linear temperature–gas-energy relation. The discrete FLD residual, Jacobian, hydro, and convergence thresholds are unchanged. XD build job **338739** completed `0:0` in 1:41; ideal binary SHA-256 `56d379d96cea55cb50497ccc4a7ea9a10897784f82f8f48a6b9817671b62993d`.

The A/B starts from the *same untouched c1090 restart*, uses the same M-debug 1 node / 64 ranks, input controls, and 110 G request. Control job **338745** with the original binary reproduced the c1095 failure (`EXIT=255`, 8 Newton iterations, gas max `0.99999981543471994`, FGMRES max true `1.79815e-8`). Repaired job **338740** completed c1090→1100 (`COMPLETED 0:0`, elapsed 3:18): 14/14 reported Newton solves converged, 30 FGMRES solves / 310 iterations, max true `1.61693e-8`, no gas failure, and `NR_LOCAL_GAS` residual-over-scale around `5e-16` in the logged block. Gas mass at c1100 was unchanged at the 15-digit HST precision; ρmax=`9.423221097381628e-11`, Tmax=`607.3709 K`, Jeans minimum `41.8178` cells.

The accepted c1090 ideal and table-EOS c1060 snapshots have maximum densities `9.1993e-11` and `9.4701e-11 g/cm³` (2.9% difference). Their central maximum temperatures are 600.6 and 416.8 K; shock candidates are 6.775–7.141 and 7.507–7.874 au. Because EOS **and** opacity differ, this pair measures the combined physical-model change. Both 3D snapshots have zero EOS-table out-of-domain cells where applicable. The ideal temperature analyzer uses the pgen's `8.3144621e7 erg K^-1 mol^-1` gas constant and μ=2.381; using a proton mass instead would introduce an artificial 0.73% temperature offset.

**Important step-level caveat found in the accepted repaired trajectory:** The 15-digit HST gives `P_min=1e-19 dyn/cm²` (the configured `hydro/pfloor=1e-10` in code units) and `Jeans_min≈1.30e-6` cells at c1095–1097. The c1095 3D snapshot locates exactly eight pressure-floor cells on the finest level at radius 6.887 au, around the accretion shock, with ρ=`7.826–7.860e-13 g/cm³`. Their combined mass is `1.289e26 g = 6.48e-8 M☉`, `5.67e-8` of the box gas mass. They are core gas (ambient tracer ~0), not boundary heat-sink cells. The floor disappears by c1098; final c1140 has no pressure-floor cells. The ten-cycle segment gate missed this three-step event, so **the full repaired trajectory cannot be claimed to maintain the Jeans ≥16 criterion on every accepted step**. This is a localized transient, but it is a real numerical artifact. The three same-restart controls below did not remove it; no control toggle was retained in the production input.

The floor appears at nearly the same physical time in all four runs. Halving CFL changes its duration in step count, not its presence. Disabling mixed-frame transport or the Riemann radiation-pressure option does not eliminate it. These controls therefore do not identify a validated physics-toggle fix. The final c1140 3D state is floor-free, but a claim of an entirely floor-free ideal-EOS path would be false. The next targeted diagnostic would record the eight cells' gas energy immediately before and after hydro flux update and implicit FLD update, then compare the pressure/energy floors used by the ideal and table EOS branches; no speculative floor clamp was added here.

It quotes approximately `29.09 kyr` and a `6–7 au` first-core shock at the first snapshot above the same density threshold. The matched ideal calculation reaches the threshold approximately 53 years later, with a shock radius of the same order. Its radial temperature is materially lower than the Figure 7 approximate raster read-offs: at 1 au about `0.70×`, at 100 au about `0.62×`; density at 1 au is about `1.01×` the approximate read-off. Those figure points are *not* an authoritative numerical table, and the codes/mesh/ambient implementations differ, so this is a physical-model comparison rather than a pointwise acceptance test. The same-code table-EOS/Semenov full path at c1080 reaches `1.0441e-10 g/cm³`, Tmax `434.5 K`, and a `7.507–7.874 au` shock, consistent with the EOS/opacity bundle changing the central temperature and shock location.
