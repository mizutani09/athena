# WOR-102: Mach 5 conservative AMR FLD A/B (2026-11-06)

## Decision

The opt-in conservative interface operator passed the local extension and the
XD2000 N2048-equivalent Mach 5 restart tests. Keep it available as an
experimental candidate. Do not enable it by default yet: the earlier local
optical-depth sweep found cases where its one-step profile error increased
(up to 2.10 times the existing stencil at N64), and the XD comparison covers
only 10 late cycles rather than a full physical evolution. This is a decision
about default adoption, not a failure of the interface conservation test.

The Newton flux limiter remains fixed during each solve. The Mach 5 problem
sets `force_lambda_one_third=true`; the transverse reconstruction is enabled
but its correction is zero for this quasi-1D profile. The nonflat Test C
combined probe below exercises that reconstruction. Coupled runs use the
assembled frozen Schur operator with FGMRES/MG: the earlier coupled secant Jv
failed the tight linear tolerance, so the original difference-Jv proposal is
not claimed to be complete for coupled production runs.

## Local gates

The local Mach 5 build used MPI/HDF5 and the actual coupled, mixed-frame,
physical-boundary pgen. Base N32 with three AMR levels and 4 ranks completed
three cycles; 85 final blocks. The existing face stencil had a maximum
coarse/fine relative mismatch of **8.05%**, whereas the candidate had
**3.12e-13**; serial and 4-rank candidate results agreed. N64/4-rank one-step
candidate mismatch was **8.31e-15**. All nonzero-step Newton solves were
strictly converged. A 100-cycle N32/4-rank candidate run actually changed
topology (112 blocks created, 28 destroyed; 92 final blocks), with all 100
Newton solves converged, maximum final L2 **3.40e-16**. Restarting that state
on 4 ranks for one more ledger cycle gave a maximum coarse/fine mismatch of
**2.31e-16**. The restart must use its checkpoint's mesh settings; overriding
them with an unrelated input is invalid.

The nonflat Test C combined trial used variable limiter values fixed during
each Newton solve, transverse amplitude 0.3, density ratio 1.15, opacity
0.004, gas/radiation coupling, tangential reconstruction, dynamic derefinement,
and 4 ranks. It completed five cycles, derefining from 18 to 4 blocks. Newton
iteration counts were 22, 12, 4, 4, 4; maximum final L2 **4.35e-11** against
the `1e-10` threshold. Across six coarse/fine stage-faces, maximum relative
mismatch was **9.37e-13** and the summed implicit diffusion ledger was
**-2.80e-16** in the periodic domain. Earlier tests in
`wor102_conservative_20261106.md` cover x1/x2/x3, coefficient contrasts,
optical depths, fixed-limiter Jv, and positivity diagnostics.

## Physical profile

The A/B HDF profiles have identical time and spatial grid. At 10 cycles, the
largest candidate-minus-baseline absolute difference within 300 cm of the
shock was **0.0435 K in Tgas** and **0.0667 K in Trad**. Both profiles remained
positive (minimum rho 5.45887e-13, minimum Tgas 99.99997 K, minimum Trad
100.00003 K). Tgas peak was 1002.237 K baseline and 1002.273 K candidate.
On that same near-shock sample, the mean absolute difference to the existing
semi-analytic reference was Tgas **1.7478 → 1.7324 K** and Trad
**1.0448 → 1.0156 K**. Thus the candidate did not visibly degrade this short
Mach 5 continuation, but this does not resolve the local optical-depth
accuracy tradeoff or establish full-run convergence.

## Next decision boundary

WOR-102's specified validation gates are met for the experimental branch.
Default adoption should require a separate accuracy decision over the thick
and moderate local cases and, if desired, a full Mach 5 continuation. N4096
resolution convergence remains under WOR-100. Do not infer total-energy
conservation or a production performance gain from this short implicit-stage
audit.
