# WOR-160: rotating SCF model to Cartesian/SMR Athena++

## Outcome

A standalone HD problem generator, `src/pgen/scf_rsg.cpp`, now imports the
axisymmetric C++ SCF solution without touching the existing preCE or NR-FLD
problem generators.  It is configured with:

```sh
python3 configure.py --prob=scf_rsg --coord=cartesian \
  --eos=adiabatic --flux=hllc --nscalars=2 --cxx=g++
make -j4
```

The supplied input is `inputs/rsg/athinput.scf_rsg_map`.  It uses a 64^3 root
grid, five nested refinement levels and a minimum cell width of
`7.47334e10 cm`.  A one-cycle initialization smoke test finishes in about one
second locally.

## Mapping definition

The SCF table is rectangular in `(r,mu)`.  `scf_axisymmetric_profile.hpp`
validates its schema and performs bilinear interpolation in those coordinates.
At a Cartesian quadrature point,

`R=sqrt(x^2+y^2)`, `r=sqrt(R^2+z^2)`, `mu=z/r`.

The model is explicitly in the inertial frame:

`v=(-Omega*y, Omega*x, 0)`.

Each leaf cell stores independent tensor-Gauss averages of
`rho`, `rho*v`, and

`E = P/(gamma-1) + rho |v|^2/2`.

This conserves the chosen ideal-HD representation, including unresolved
subcell rotational kinetic energy.  Consequently the pressure reconstructed
from cell-averaged conserved variables is not imposed separately; its measured
L1 difference is `4.34e-6` at the recommended resolution.

Passive scalar 0 stores stellar mass and scalar 1 stores radiative-layer
stellar mass.  They distinguish atmosphere from star and preserve the layer
interface through SMR.  The equatorial 50% interface is mapped with relative
radius error `-8.89e-4`.

## Atmosphere and EOS boundary

The atmosphere is a separate, nonrotating parameter set:

- `rho_atm=1e-14 g cm^-3`;
- `T_atm=1e4 K`;
- pressure derived with the configured mean molecular weight;
- hydro density and pressure floors remain separate lower bounds.

The SCF pressure is the piecewise model's total pressure.  WOR-160 treats that
quantity as the pressure of a gamma-law HD fluid so that density, pressure,
momentum and an unambiguous HD energy can be mapped and audited.  The source
gas+radiation temperature is exported as a diagnostic user variable, but the
gamma-law evolution cannot preserve source `P`, `rho`, and gas+radiation `T`
simultaneously.  Thermodynamically consistent FLD initialization is explicitly
deferred to WOR-163.

## Quantitative audit

| grid | min dx/h | mass error | discrete Jz error | HD energy error | force L2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| uniform 32^3 | 13.51 | -3.56e-3 | -2.33e-4 | -1.60e-1 | 3.41e-1 |
| root 32 + L4 | 0.844 | -5.18e-5 | 8.27e-4 | 1.42e-3 | 3.08e-1 |
| root 32 + L6 | 0.211 | -2.11e-5 | 8.27e-4 | 2.98e-3 | 6.91e-2 |
| root 64 + L4 | 0.422 | -2.21e-5 | -5.06e-6 | 2.94e-3 | 2.25e-1 |
| root 64 + L5 | 0.211 | -2.18e-5 | -5.03e-6 | 2.98e-3 | 6.91e-2 |

Resolving only the core does not converge angular momentum because most `Jz`
is carried by the outer rotating envelope.  Root-grid refinement is therefore
required independently of central nested refinement.  Conversely, the large
uniform-grid energy error is core-pressure aliasing and disappears once `h`
is resolved.

The remaining 6.9% mapped force L2 is a handoff diagnostic, not a hidden pass.
It combines Cartesian pressure differencing with the imported continuous
potential, before Athena++ gravity and surface treatment are made consistent.
WOR-161 should use this field and the high local surface maximum to evaluate
the discrete source, boundary and floor choices.

## Reproduction and tests

The independent C++ auditor is `tools/rsg/scf_smr_map_audit.cpp`.  It emits the
mass, atmosphere mass, exact quadrature and Athena-discrete angular momentum,
energy components, primitive reconstruction errors and force residuals.

`tests/scf_mapping/test_mapping.py` compiles and runs the C++ loader unit test,
executes the recommended composite audit, and optionally launches the compiled
Athena++ pgen.  The acceptance thresholds are mass `<5e-5`, discrete Jz
`<1e-4`, HD energy `<5e-3`, pressure reconstruction `<1e-5`, and force L2
`<0.1`.

No gravitational source, damping, artificial preparation heating or FLD term
is enabled in this issue.  A one-cycle run verifies initialization and I/O
only; physical relaxation belongs to WOR-161/162.
